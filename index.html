Honest limits. The expression net is a compact model trained on posed, lab-style faces (FER-2013 class of datasets). It is good at strong, frontal, well-lit expressions and weak on subtle or mixed ones - "neutral" and "sad" are often close, and reading glasses, beards, masks or side angles degrade it. Treat readings as a demo of the model's guesses, never as a person's real feelings. All inference is local; no frame or photo leaves your browser.
